Denver DJ & MC services

Voted Westword's Best of Denver 2026. The DJ company Denver keeps rehiring for weddings, corporate events, and parties.

Moon Magnet DJ Productions has been packing Denver dancefloors since 2013. With back-to-back Best of Zola wins and five-star ratings on every platform, we bring DJ, MC, sound, and lighting to your event — and every booking includes expert MCing, because the music is only half the job.

We all know those parties where the music just isn't right — people keep drifting off the dance floor and the DJ can't read the room. Even if the food, venue, and guest list are perfect, the entertainment can still fall flat. Our DJs have spent over a decade handling the moments nobody can plan for: dinner running long, a grandmother requesting a song in Spanish, a dance floor that needs to empty and fill again in a moment's notice.

Before your event, you get unlimited planning meetings and an online music planner — tell us your must-plays and do-not-plays, or tell us to take the lead. Whatever you're planning, we've likely done it before: weddings, kids parties, prom and school dances, quinceañeras, corporate events, Bar Mitzvahs, and even live accordion add-ons.
